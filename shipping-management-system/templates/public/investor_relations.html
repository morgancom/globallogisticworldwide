About Global shippments Group

Global shippments Group is the world's leading logistics provider. We connect people and markets and enable global trade. Our strategy is built around three priorities: being the first choice for customers, employees and investors; leading in green logistics; and accelerating digital transformation across every division.

We focus on profitable growth in our core logistics businesses and disciplined capital allocation. With approximately 500,000 employees in over 220 countries and territories, we generate revenues of more than €80 billion annually.

The Group is organised into five operating divisions: Express, Global Forwarding & Freight, Supply Chain, eCommerce, and Post & Parcel.
