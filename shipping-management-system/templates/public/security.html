Best practices

What you can do to stay secure

Security is a shared responsibility. Here are the practices we recommend for individuals and organisations using our platform.

For individual users

  • Use a strong, unique password of at least 12 characters — never reuse it elsewhere
  • Enable two-factor authentication on your Global shippments account
  • Never share your login credentials by email, phone or chat
  • Always sign out of shared or public devices
  • Review your account activity regularly and report anything unusual
  • Keep your recovery email and phone number up to date

For organisations

  • Implement role-based access control across your Global shippments team accounts
  • Schedule regular security audits and independent penetration tests
  • Run security awareness training for everyone who manages shipments
  • Rotate API keys at least every 90 days
  • Restrict sensitive operations to whitelisted IP ranges where possible
  • Enable automated threat detection and connect alerts to your SIEM
Under the hood

Advanced security features

A closer look at the technical controls we operate across our infrastructure.

Firewall protection

Enterprise-grade firewalls with intrusion prevention systems monitor traffic at every layer of our infrastructure.

DDoS mitigation

Multi-layer DDoS protection keeps services available during volumetric and application-layer attacks.

VPN access

All administrative access is routed through a hardened VPN with per-session authentication.

Network segmentation

Strict segmentation between environments limits the potential blast radius of any incident.

Encryption at rest

AES-256 encryption on all stored data, including database backups and cold storage.

Encryption in transit

TLS 1.3 with perfect forward secrecy on every request, internal and external.

Data masking

Sensitive fields are automatically masked in application logs and non-production environments.

Backup encryption

Backups use separate key management with restricted, audited access.

Multi-factor authentication

MFA is required for all administrative accounts and optional-but-recommended for customers.

Single sign-on

SAML 2.0 and OAuth 2.0 support for enterprise identity providers.

Privileged access

Just-in-time elevation with automatic approval workflows and short-lived credentials.

Session management

Automatic session timeout, concurrent session limits and re-authentication for sensitive actions.
