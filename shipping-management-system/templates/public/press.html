About Global shippments Group

Global shippments is the world's leading logistics provider. Our divisions offer an unrivalled portfolio of services — from national and international parcel delivery to e-commerce fulfilment, international express, road, air and ocean transport, and industrial supply-chain management.

With approximately 500,000 employees in more than 220 countries and territories, we connect people and businesses securely and reliably, enabling global sustainable trade flows.

Global shippments Group generated revenues of more than €80 billion in the most recent fiscal year, and is committed to net-zero emissions by 2050.
