01

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, book a shipment, contact support, or interact with our digital services. We also collect certain information automatically when you use our website or mobile applications.

  • Account details — full name, email address, phone number and login credentials
  • Shipping information — sender and recipient addresses, contact details, and delivery preferences
  • Payment information — processed securely by PCI-DSS-compliant payment providers; we never store full card numbers
  • Shipment details — contents, dimensions, weight, declared value and any customs documentation
  • Communication preferences — how you would like to receive updates and marketing messages
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device identifiers and pages visited, collected via cookies and similar technologies
We do not collect sensitive personal information such as political opinions, religious beliefs, health data or biometric identifiers.
02

How we use your information

We use your personal information only where we have a legal basis to do so — typically to perform our contract with you, to comply with a legal obligation, or based on our legitimate business interests. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Provide and manage our services — process bookings, deliver shipments and provide customer support
  • Send transactional updates — tracking notifications, delivery confirmations and account alerts by email or SMS
  • Comply with legal obligations — customs documentation, tax records and regulatory reporting
  • Improve our services — analyse usage patterns, fix issues and develop new features
  • Prevent fraud and abuse — detect and prevent fraudulent activity and secure our systems
  • Send marketing communications — only where you have opted in; you can unsubscribe at any time
03

Information sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Sharing is limited to the parties needed to deliver our services, and only under strict contractual data-protection obligations:

  • Delivery and logistics partners — carriers, customs brokers and last-mile providers in our network
  • Service providers — IT, payment, analytics and customer support vendors acting on our instructions
  • Customs and regulatory authorities — where required by law for international shipments
  • Law enforcement and courts — in response to lawful requests, legal process or to protect our rights
  • Group companies — Global shippments Group entities that support our operations under internal data-protection rules

All third parties are bound by written agreements that require them to protect your personal information to the same standards we apply internally.

04

Data security

We implement technical and organisational measures designed to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction:

  • 256-bit TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Encryption at rest for sensitive databases
  • Multi-factor authentication for account access
  • 24/7 security monitoring and intrusion detection
  • Regular audits and independent penetration testing
  • Employee training on data protection and privacy

Where a personal data breach affects your rights or freedoms, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority and you without undue delay, in accordance with applicable law.

05

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) or similar laws:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing — limit how we use your information
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests, including profiling
  • Right to withdraw consent — at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing
  • Right to lodge a complaint — with your local data-protection supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer using the details below. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law — typically within 30 days.
