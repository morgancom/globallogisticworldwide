Our story

Built on a simple idea: speed that matters.

Global shippments was founded in 1969 on the belief that businesses deserved a faster, more reliable way to move goods across borders. What began as a small operation handling urgent documents between San Francisco and Honolulu has grown into one of the world's most extensive logistics networks.

Today we operate across five integrated divisions — Express, Global forwarding, Supply chain, eCommerce and Post & parcel — serving multinationals, growing retailers, governments and families with the same standard of care. Every shipment is tracked in real time, every route is optimised by data, and every customer has a direct line to a real person.

Our journey

Five decades of logistics milestones

From a single trans-Pacific route to a network spanning every continent.

1969

The beginning

Founded as a small express service moving urgent documents between San Francisco and Honolulu — pioneering the idea of door-to-door priority shipping.

1970s

International expansion

Rapid growth into the Far East, the Pacific Rim, the Middle East, Africa and Europe — establishing the first global framework for our network.

1998

Global consolidation

Full integration into a worldwide logistics group, adding air, ocean and supply chain capabilities under one roof — creating the integrated partner we are today.

2012

Digital transformation

Launched a multi-year programme to connect every shipment to real-time tracking, digitise customs documentation and modernise our warehousing infrastructure.

2025

Roadmap to net zero

Committed to a multi-billion euro investment in clean operations — electric last-mile fleets, sustainable aviation fuel and climate-neutral facilities — targeting net-zero emissions by 2050.
